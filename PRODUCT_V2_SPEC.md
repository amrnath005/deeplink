---

## 1. PRODUCT POSITIONING

DeepLink Pro is the **Mobile Campaign Intelligence Platform** built specifically for mobile-first e-commerce and D2C brands. 

Instead of acting as a generic URL shortener or an esoteric research sandbox, DeepLink Pro guarantees that every mobile campaign click—from Instagram Ads, TikTok Influencer links, paid social, and QR codes—seamlessly launches target native apps or mobile web storefronts while capturing actionable traffic quality scoring and end-to-end conversion attribution.

### The Three Core Product Pillars:
1. **⚡ SMART ROUTING:** Sub-20ms edge resolution, webview-aware app scheme launchers, iOS Universal Links, Android App Links, dynamic QR codes, and custom short domain management.
2. **🛡️ TRAFFIC QUALITY:** Instant bot/crawler detection, device & network entropy risk scoring, proxy/VPN flags, and suspicious click suppression.
3. **📊 ATTRIBUTION:** End-to-end UTM campaign tracking, conversion event ingestion (purchases, add-to-carts, signups), first-party attribution modeling, and Webhook dispatch to ad networks.

## 2. IDEAL CUSTOMER PROFILE (ICP)

### Primary ICP: Mobile-First E-Commerce & D2C Brands
* **Target Roles:** VP of Growth, Performance Marketing Managers, Head of Acquisition, E-Commerce Operations Directors at D2C brands ($500K to $20M ARR).
* **Primary Channels:** Instagram Ads, TikTok Creator Campaigns, Meta Ads, Paid Search, Email SMS links, Physical Product Packaging QR Codes.
* **Core Pain Point:** High ad spend wasted when in-app webviews block checkout logins, inability to distinguish real human buyers from bot traffic, and fragmented campaign conversion tracking across mobile web and native apps.

### Explicit Non-Goals (Do NOT Optimize For):
* ❌ Creators & Influencers as the primary ICP (no generic link-in-bio page builders).
* ❌ Generic consumer URL shortening (not competing with basic Bitly utilities).
* ❌ Event ticketing or sneaker drop platforms as primary focus.
* ❌ Autonomous AI shopping agents or agent-to-agent bidding protocols.
* ❌ Cryptographic ZK-SNARK zero-knowledge research.
* ❌ Biometric passkey drop gating.

## 3. CORE USER PROBLEMS

1. **In-App Webview Conversion Friction:** Mobile ad clicks inside Instagram/TikTok open in restrictive embedded browsers where saved Google/Apple passwords and payment methods are unavailable, causing drop-off before checkout.
2. **Unvalidated Traffic Quality:** Ad networks charge for clicks generated by headless bots, scrapers, or click farms without marketers realizing which campaigns drive fake traffic.
3. **Broken Attribution Loops:** Disconnect between top-of-funnel ad clicks and down-funnel purchase conversions across mobile web and native mobile apps.
4. **Fragile Link Infrastructure:** Custom shortlinks breaking during domain migrations or failing to launch native iOS/Android apps reliably.

## 7. V1 SCOPE (CORE RELEASE)

1. **Smart Link Management:** Create, edit, list, and delete campaign shortlinks with custom aliases and optional expiry.
2. **Webview-Aware Smart Routing Engine:** Sub-20ms edge resolution supporting YouTube, Spotify, Telegram, Instagram, and web schemes with browser breakout heuristics.
3. **Official Mobile App Link Hosting:** Serving valid `/.well-known/apple-app-site-association` and `/.well-known/assetlinks.json` domain association payloads.
4. **UTM & Campaign Tracking:** Standardized parsing and retention of `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_term`, and `utm_content`.
5. **Traffic Quality Scoring Engine:** Lightweight risk scoring evaluating bot user-agents, headless drivers, and IP proxies (`botScore: 0.00` to `1.00`).
6. **Conversion Event Ingestion API (`POST /api/events`):** Ingest purchase, checkout, and signup conversion events linked to shortlink clicks.
7. **First-Party Campaign Analytics Dashboard (`/dashboard`):** Clean campaign performance view showing Clicks, Conversions, Conversion Rate, Traffic Quality Risk, and Device/OS distribution.
8. **Vector QR Code Suite:** Downloadable SVG/PNG QR code generator for print and physical product campaigns.
9. **Custom Domain Support:** Domain mapping configuration for branded short domains (`dplk.pro`).
10. **REST API & Webhooks (`POST /api/webhooks`):** Programmatic link creation and real-time conversion webhook dispatch.

---

## 8. V2 SCOPE (EXTENDED RELEASE)

1. **Deferred Deep Linking Engine:** First-launch attribution passing parameters through App Store installs.
2. **Deferred Cart Recovery:** Pre-filling mobile app cart items after App Store downloads.
3. **Single-Source Affiliate Deduplication:** Multi-network deduplication preventing double affiliate commission payouts.
4. **Native Mobile SDKs:** iOS Swift & Android Kotlin client libraries for MMP event logging.
5. **Advanced Bot Fraud Shield:** Automated pixel suppression and ad network integration.
6. **Multi-Team Workspaces:** Multi-tenant team roles, permissions, and organization workspaces.

## 11. SUCCESS METRICS

1. **Routing Speed:** 100% of smart link redirects resolve in `< 50ms`.
2. **App Launch Reliability:** 100% valid URI scheme generation for supported mobile apps.
3. **Traffic Quality Accuracy:** Zero false positives on verified human desktop/mobile browsers.
4. **Conversion Attribution Match Rate:** `> 95%` attribution match between campaign click tokens and ingested purchase events within a 30-day window.
5. **Core UI Clarity:** Clean 3-pillar navigation (**Smart Routing**, **Traffic Quality**, **Attribution**).

---

## 12. PRODUCT RISKS

1. **Browser Webview Policy Changes:** Meta or ByteDance altering in-app browser intent scheme handling on iOS/Android.
2. **Apple ITP & Privacy Restriction Updates:** Changes to iOS Safari IP masking impacting geolocation and risk scoring accuracy.
3. **Third-Party QR API Dependability:** External QR generation services; resolved by relying on native local QR vector generation.

---

## 13. COMPETITIVE ASSUMPTIONS THAT REQUIRE VALIDATION

1. **D2C Brand Adoption:** D2C brand growth teams prioritize webview breakout and traffic quality over generic link shortening features.
2. **Attribution Preference:** Growth marketers prefer a lightweight first-party campaign conversion API over heavy legacy MMP software (e.g. Branch/AppsFlyer) for web-to-app flows.
3. **Pricing Elasticity:** D2C brands are willing to pay $29/mo to $299/mo for campaign routing, traffic quality, and conversion tracking.
